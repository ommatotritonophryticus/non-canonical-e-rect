
Playing
-------
Waves of rectangles come at you from both sides of a field that runs on
forever left and right; the view follows you, and enemies come to wherever you
are, so running does not lose them for long. Clear a wave and there is a short
lull and a countdown before the next.

    jump      off the ground
    attack    swing whatever you are carrying; three swings in quick
              succession make a combo, and the third reaches twice as far
    dash      a short burst that shoves what it passes through and cannot be
              touched while it lasts; it costs a third of your energy bar
    slam      in the air: drop fast and put up the wall on landing, if you
              hold a charge - the wall hits everything it reaches

Hitting and killing fills the energy bar, and a full bar becomes a wall charge
(the number at the top of the screen). Wounded players bank energy faster - up
to three times as fast on the brink.

Upgrades
--------
Every 4000 points the team scores, three boxes stand in the lull. Hit one to
take it. A swing that reaches more than one takes the nearest in front of you;
starting the next wave early turns the offer down.

Weapons - one at a time, each up to level 3. The third level, MASTER, changes
what the weapon does rather than adding more of it. A different weapon arrives
one level below the one you carry; from wave 26, at its top level.

    BASIC     what you start with; the third hit of a combo leaves a blast
              that hurts everything around it
    HAMMER    half the reach and a slow swing, but one heavy blow per enemy
              that throws it twice as far
    PIERCE    does not throw, so an enemy stays in the swing and keeps taking
              it. While an enemy is within its reach the whole game slows
              down - to a quarter of its speed when it is right against you
    LUNGE     starts short and reaches out through the swing. It does not
              throw what it catches either: it carries it out on its tip, and
              the enemy walks back in once the swing is over
    SINGLE    one guaranteed hit per enemy per swing
    SWEEP     a low strip out of both sides at once; flyers and jumpers pass
              above it
    HEAVY     shorter, but tall enough to cover you top to bottom
    SHOT      throws a square that stops on what it hits; hold attack to keep
              firing
    TRAP      places boxes that stay where they are put and hurt whatever
              walks into them

Boons - kept for the rest of the run. The ones marked 2 are a second level,
offered only once the first is held.

    DOUBLE JUMP       one jump in the air, back after the next landing
    DOUBLE JUMP 2     it also comes back when you are hit or caught in a blast
    FREE DASH         no cooldown on the dash; only its energy is paid
    FREE DASH 2       and that costs a fifth of what it did
    SHIELD            takes one hit for you, then needs a second to come back
    SHIELD 2          the hit it takes goes off as a blast around you
    BLACK WALL        the wall drags every enemy toward you
    GREY WALL         the wall throws every enemy away from you
    AUTO ATTACK       hold attack to keep swinging, with a short rest between
                      swings (not offered while you carry SHOT, which already
                      does)

From wave 26 there is an offer after every wave, and two of its three boxes
are terms for the coming wave instead - they say THIS WAVE, and they last for
that wave only.

Modes
-----
    1 PLAYER, 2 PLAYERS   the endless run; it ends when you do. Two players
                          share every offer: what one takes, both get.
    WEAPON RUNS           one weapon, from the start to an end:

        TUTORIAL      three waves, and the weapon goes up a level every wave
        CAMPAIGN      only that weapon and the boons are ever offered; the
                      run is won one wave after the weapon reaches MASTER
        HEAD START    a campaign that starts on wave 7, with 5000 points and
                      the weapon already in hand. Locked for each weapon until
                      you have taken that weapon in its campaign.

The weapon-runs screen shows how far you have got with each weapon in each
mode.

Waves
-----
Every fifth wave is a boss wave. Wave 10 belongs to a single flying boss: twice
the size of an ordinary flyer, and it blinks away every time you land a hit.
Wave 15 can bring a boss that sheds a husk every time it is hit, and wave 20
has a boss of its own.

A wave can carry up to two modifiers, one of each kind, announced during the
countdown:

    who turns up      GROUND ONLY, FLYERS ONLY, HORDE, JUMPERS
    how it plays      HOLD THE LINE  view is pinned, you start on full health
                      GROUNDED       no jumping; down raises the wall, longer reach
                      NO WALL        the slam buys invulnerability; landing heals
                      BLIND          you cannot see them, they can barely hurt you
                      NO FOOTING     you cannot walk; every step is a jump or a
                                     dash

No blast ever hurts you - it only throws you up. Red blinkers leave one and
reappear at the edge of the field when touched, and once more the first time
you hit one. Shooters show a white sight that turns red for the last 100 ms
before they fire - that is the moment to step out of the line.

Music and effect volumes are in Settings.
